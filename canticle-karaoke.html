Canticle Karaoke Support

Canticle Karaoke turns a song recording into a karaoke presentation. Lyrics highlight word by word in sync with the music, so no one has to run slides. Your audio stays on your device.

How to get help

Email support: gundeck@me.com

We read support email for sync, playback, presentation, and bug questions. Include your app version, iPhone or iPad model, and iOS version when you write about a bug.

You can also open a note on GitHub: New support issue.

FAQ

How does the highlighting work? Import a song, add or confirm the lyrics, and the app generates word timing for that recording. During presentation, each word highlights as it is sung.

The highlight looks off. What helps? Make sure the lyrics match the exact recording you imported, including verses and repeats. Editing the lyrics and running sync again usually fixes drift.

Where does my audio go? Nowhere. Your song files stay on your device. Sync and playback happen on the device.

Can I present on a bigger screen? Yes. Use the presentation view full screen, or connect your device to a TV or projector the way you normally share your screen.

Do I need an account? No. Canticle Karaoke does not require an account.
