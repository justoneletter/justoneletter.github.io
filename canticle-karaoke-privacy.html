Canticle Karaoke Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026. Canticle Karaoke is made by Christopher Mosley (“we,” “us”).

Summary

We do not sell your personal information. Canticle Karaoke does not require an account, and your audio stays on your device.

Audio and lyrics

Song files you import, and the lyrics and timing created for them, are stored locally on your device. Audio is not uploaded to us or to any server we operate. Lyrics and word timing are generated on your device.

Support email

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include in that email, such as your email address and any screenshots you attach. We use it only to respond to you and to fix problems. Please do not send song files you do not have the rights to share.

Analytics and tracking

We do not sell your data and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. If analytics are added later, this policy will be updated before they collect personal data.

Children

Canticle Karaoke is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit personal information of children under 13 to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
