Ingredient Q Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026. Ingredient Q is made by Christopher Mosley (“we,” “us”).

Summary

We do not sell your personal information. Ingredient Q does not require an account.

Information on your device

Scans, lists, questions, and similar content you create in Ingredient Q are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives or stores that content.

Support email

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include in that email, such as your email address and any screenshots you attach. We use it only to respond to you and to fix problems.

Analytics and tracking

We do not sell your data and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. If analytics are added later, this policy will be updated before they collect personal data.

Children

Ingredient Q is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit personal information of children under 13 to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
