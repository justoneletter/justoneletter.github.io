Together privacy policy

Effective October 22, 2026. Together is published by Christopher Mosley (“we” or “us”).

Together provides private reflection tools and an optional shared partner space. This policy explains the information used by the current native iOS app and the controls available to you.

Information used

The app uses your chosen display name, iCloud-derived user identity, the email you enter to authorize a partner invitation, private reflection text, selected shared text, and readiness and pause information to provide its features. Freeform answers may contain personal information that you choose to enter.

There are no advertising or analytics SDKs in this build. The app does not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell your personal information.

Storage on your device and in iCloud

Pending private reflections are stored in protected Application Support files on your device and excluded from device backup by the app. Choosing Sync private reflections uploads eligible pending entries to your personal CloudKit private database. Device-only drafts are associated with the verified iCloud account before upload. Drafts already associated with another account are excluded.

Partner sharing uses a separate CloudKit record zone, accessible through a private invitation. Private original writing is not added to that partner zone automatically. Shared writing is held in memory by the app and is cleared when shared access cannot be verified. The app may also use iCloud to sync your chosen display name and to keep your private willingness and capacity answers outside the partner share.

Apple operates iCloud and CloudKit under its applicable terms and privacy policy. Together does not claim end-to-end encryption for CloudKit records. Internet access is required for confirmed cloud changes.

Information shared with your partner

The app invites one named partner. They can read selected shared copies, shared check-in notes, your chosen display name, derived readiness, readiness expiry, and pause state. Your numeric capacity and private willingness answers are stored outside the partner zone. The app does not show private journaling activity or read receipts.

Read/write participants have CloudKit permission to modify the shared zone. App controls restrict normal changes to the author’s own entries, but the shared space is not an immutable evidence log. If a CloudKit owner expands a share outside the app, that permission may expose existing shared records. Together blocks its shared workflow when extra participants are detected, but the owner must remove the extra access or end the share.

Your controls and retention

You can export your private writing, delete individual entries, withdraw a shared copy, and disconnect a shared space. Confirmed deletion or disconnection updates iCloud when online. The owner can end the shared space for everyone; an invited participant can leave it. Original private writing remains separate from the shared copy.

Copies another person has already read, copied, or exported cannot be recalled. Uninstalling removes the app’s local files; cloud records may remain until deleted through the app or Apple’s iCloud controls. Account changes hide the prior account’s shared data and isolate its pending drafts. We do not set an automatic expiry for your reflection records.

Support and these web pages

If you email support, the publisher receives your email address and the information you choose to include so that your question can be answered. Please avoid sending private journal entries, partner information, passwords, or invitation links. You can contact us about access to or deletion of information you sent to support.

These static support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection and request information under its privacy statement. These pages have no added analytics, contact forms, or tracking scripts.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s privacy practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date.
