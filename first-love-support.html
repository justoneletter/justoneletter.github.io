First Love Support

Help for First Love: Jesus First, published by Christopher Mosley.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, or feedback: gundeck@me.com. Please include your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

Do I need an account?

No. First Love has no account or sign-in. Open the app and begin.

Where is my data stored?

Your journal entries, reflections, relationship notes, and progress are stored only on your device. We do not run a cloud journal or sync service, so we cannot see or recover your entries.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app from your device deletes your journal and everything else First Love stored there. This cannot be undone.

How do I manage or cancel First Love+?

First Love+ subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name (Apple ID), then Subscriptions, and choose First Love. You can change or cancel your plan there at any time. Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues until the end of the current period.

I already subscribed. How do I get First Love+ back?

Open First Love, go to the First Love+ screen, and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.
