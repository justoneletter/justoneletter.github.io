Metanoia Support

Metanoia is a quiet place to pray, journal, and return your attention to God. Prayer stays free. Digital Metanoia is an optional Apple In-App Purchase.

How to get help

Email support: gundeck@me.com

We read support email for account, purchase, Screen Time / Digital Metanoia, and bug questions.

You can also open a note on GitHub: New support issue.

FAQ

Is prayer free? Yes. Prayer, journal, and Today’s Anchor stay free.

What is Digital Metanoia? An optional layer for gentle Screen Time guardrails, unlocked with Apple In-App Purchase (Monthly, Yearly, or Lifetime).

How do I restore purchases? Use Restore Purchases in the Digital Metanoia / paywall screen while signed into the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel a subscription? On your iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Metanoia. Or manage at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

Where is my journal? On your device only. We do not host your journal on a server.
