Short answers about Just One Letter, Metanoia Prayer, and First Love: Jesus First. For a fuller picture, start on the home page.

What is Just One Letter?

Just One Letter is an independent creative studio founded by Christopher Mosley. It makes apps and ideas that help people pray, reflect, and reconnect. The name comes from a change in Christopher's own name: Chris + t = Christ. The "t" points to the cross, and Christopher means "bearer of Christ."

Is there a Christian prayer app from Just One Letter?

Yes. Metanoia Prayer is a quiet Christian iOS app to pray, reflect, and begin again. It is not a streak and not a score. You can start with Today's Anchor, a verse and a short reflection for the day. There are also Prayer Moments across the day, guided prayer (including Structured Prayer and Contemplative Prayer), Return to Sender, a silent sit, and a private journal that stays on your device. Prayer stays free.

Metanoia Prayer is published by Christopher Mosley. It is not the same as other apps that also use the name Metanoia, such as confession or Mass-reading apps from other developers.

What is First Love: Jesus First? Is it a dating app?

First Love: Jesus First is a Jesus-first iOS app that helps Christians return to Jesus as first love, so earthly relationships can be honest and lasting. It is not a dating app, and it is not therapy.

You can practice Daily Anchors in Scripture, walk Guided Settling Paths, use Thought Screen for structured reflection, and keep a private journal. Everything you write stays on your device.

Are the apps free?

Yes. Both apps are free to download on the App Store, and the core practices stay free.

In Metanoia Prayer, prayer, the journal, and Today's Anchor stay free. Digital Metanoia is an optional Apple In-App Purchase.

In First Love, everyone gets Daily Anchors, core teaching, 3 Thought Screens a week, and the first 3 steps of each path. First Love+ is an optional subscription that opens every path and unlimited Thought Screens. The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers.

Prices are listed on the App Store and may vary by region. Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple.

Do Metanoia Prayer and First Love work on Android?

Not at this time. Both apps are on the iOS App Store. Metanoia Prayer requires iOS 16.6 or later. First Love: Jesus First requires iOS 15.0 or later. Both also run on iPad, and on a Mac with Apple silicon.

Does Just One Letter collect my prayers or journal entries?

No. In both apps, your journal and reflections stay on your device. There is no account or sign-in, and there is no cloud journal. The App Store privacy labels state that neither app collects data. Deleting the app from your device deletes what was stored there.

How do I get the apps, or get in touch?

You can find both apps on Christopher Mosley's App Store page, or through the links on the home page.

For questions, ideas, app support, or purchases, write to hello@just1letter.com.

What else is the studio working on?

Two more apps, Stillpoint Reflection and Together, are taking shape and are not released yet. Canticle is a worship lyric display in build now, made so a small church can present lyrics in time with the song without someone running the slides. It is not yet released.

More projects will be added as they take shape. The apps are introduced on the home page.
