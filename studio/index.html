A note from Christopher

One letter. A cross.
A new beginning.

Just One Letter began with a change in my own name: Chris + t = Christ. The “t” points to the cross, and Christopher means “bearer of Christ.” It became a way to express my turn toward Jesus.

That is the heart I want to carry into this studio: apps and ideas that help people pray, reflect, and reconnect.

ChristopherFounder, Just One Letter
