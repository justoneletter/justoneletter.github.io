Ingredient Q Support

Ingredient Q helps you look closer at ingredient lists and get clear answers about what is in the products you use every day.

How to get help

Email support: gundeck@me.com

We read support email for questions, corrections, and bug reports. Include your app version, iPhone or iPad model, and iOS version when you write about a bug.

You can also open a note on GitHub: New support issue.

FAQ

Does Ingredient Q give medical or dietary advice? No. Ingredient Q provides information only. It is not medical advice. Talk with a qualified professional about your own health, allergies, or diet.

Do I need an account? No. Ingredient Q does not require an account.

Where is my information stored? On your device. We do not host your scans, lists, or questions on a server.

How do I delete my information? Delete the app from your device. That removes the information the app stored on that device.

Something looks wrong. What should I do? Email support with the product and ingredient involved, and what you expected to see. Screenshots help.
