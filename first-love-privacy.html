First Love Privacy Policy

Effective September 21, 2026. First Love: Jesus First is published by Christopher Mosley (“we,” “us”).

Summary

First Love is a local-only Christian spiritual-formation app. We do not sell your personal information. There is no account or sign-in. Your journal and reflections stay on your device.

Information on your device

Onboarding choices, journal entries, relationship notes, Thought Screen reflections, and similar content are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a First Love cloud journal or sync service.

Purchases

Optional First Love+ subscriptions are processed by Apple through In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment details. We may receive non-sensitive purchase status (for example, whether First Love+ is active) via StoreKit on your device.

Analytics and tracking

We do not sell your data. If analytics are added later, this policy will be updated before they collect personal data.

Children

First Love is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit personal information of children under 13 to us.
