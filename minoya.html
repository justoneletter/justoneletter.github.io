Minoya Support

Support for Minoya, an app by Christopher Mosley.

How to get help

Email support: gundeck@me.com

We read support email for questions and bug reports. Include your app version, iPhone or iPad model, and iOS version when you write about a bug.

You can also open a note on GitHub: New support issue.

FAQ

Do I need an account? No. Minoya does not require an account.

Where is my information stored? On your device. We do not host your app content on a server.

How do I delete my information? Delete the app from your device. That removes the information the app stored on that device.

How do purchases and refunds work? Any purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. Refund requests go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I report a bug? Email support with what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. Screenshots help.
