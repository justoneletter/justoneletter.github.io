Together offers private reflection and a shared space for two partners. Each person uses their own Apple Account with iCloud.

Using Together

How do we connect?
Each person installs Together and signs into their own Apple Account with iCloud enabled in device Settings. In Together, one person opens Our space, enters their partner’s iCloud email address, and creates a private invitation. They send that link to the invited partner, who opens and accepts it. Partner features need an internet connection.

Where are my private reflections?
Saving a private reflection stores it on this device. Choose Sync private reflections to upload eligible pending writing to your personal iCloud account. Your private originals are kept outside the partner share.

What does my partner see?
Your linked partner sees only the copies and check-in notes you deliberately share, your chosen display name, and shared readiness and pause information. Your numeric capacity and private willingness answers stay outside the partner space.

Why can’t we begin a check-in?
Both people choose readiness, with enough capacity, before starting. Readiness expires after two hours. Either person can pause; after a pause, both choose readiness again. Pull to refresh if the shared space has changed.

What if sync fails?
Check your connection and iCloud availability in device Settings, then return to Together and refresh. An on-device pending reflection is different from a confirmed iCloud save. Shared changes are confirmed only while online.

Can I remove shared words or disconnect?
You can withdraw your own shared copies and disconnect the shared space. The owner ends the share for everyone; the invited partner can leave it. Confirmed changes update iCloud when online. Copies someone already read or saved cannot be recalled. Your own private originals remain separate.

Can we add another person?
Together’s shared workflow is designed for two people. If a share is expanded outside the app, Together blocks that workflow. The owner must remove the extra access or end the share. Apple’s read/write sharing permissions apply to the whole shared zone.
